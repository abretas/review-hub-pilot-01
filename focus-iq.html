Primal Force Focus IQ Ingredients

Primal Force Focus IQ is formulated with a unique blend of active components that have been carefully selected for their proven benefits to brain health and cognitive performance. Each ingredient plays a vital role in enhancing focus, memory, and mental clarity.

Here’s a detailed look at each ingredient:

1. Acetyl L-Carnitine

  • Acetyl L-Carnitine is an amino acid that supports brain energy and cognitive performance. It has been shown to enhance mental clarity, boost focus, and improve memory recall, especially in older adults. This ingredient helps by promoting the production of acetylcholine, a neurotransmitter crucial for memory and learning.

2. Bacopa Monieri Leaf Extract

  • Bacopa Monieri is a traditional herb known for its cognitive-enhancing properties. Studies have demonstrated its ability to improve memory recall, reduce anxiety, and enhance focus. Bacopa works by supporting the brain’s neuroplasticity, helping it form and maintain new neural connections.

3. Kanna (Sceletium tortuosum)

  • Kanna, an herb native to South Africa, is known for its mood-enhancing and stress-reducing effects. It has been used for centuries in traditional medicine to alleviate stress and anxiety. Research shows that Kanna can improve emotional well-being and cognitive function by modulating serotonin levels, which help regulate mood and stress responses.

4. Ginkgo Biloba Leaf Extract (24% Flavones, 6% Lactones)

  • Ginkgo Biloba is one of the most well-known herbs for cognitive enhancement. It promotes blood circulation in the brain, which helps improve memory, mental clarity, and focus. Ginkgo’s active compounds, flavonoids and terpenoids, have been shown to protect brain cells from oxidative damage and improve neuroplasticity.

5. Gotu Kola Herb Extract 4:1

  • Gotu Kola, known as the “herb of longevity,” is used in traditional medicine for its cognitive and anti-aging benefits. This herb supports healthy neuron function and has been shown to improve memory and mental clarity. Gotu Kola promotes blood circulation, helping to deliver nutrients to the brain more efficiently.

6. Omega-3 Fatty Acid Oil (EPA, DHA)

  • Omega-3 fatty acids, particularly EPA (Eicosapentaenoic acid) and DHA (Docosahexaenoic acid), are essential fats that play a crucial role in brain health. 
  • DHA is a major structural component of the brain and is essential for maintaining neuronal function and plasticity. 
  • EPA, on the other hand, has anti-inflammatory properties and helps protect the brain from oxidative stress. Omega-3 fatty acids support memory, focus, and mental clarity, while also promoting long-term brain health.

7. Scutellaria Baicalensis Root Extract

  • Scutellaria Baicalensis, or Baikal skullcap, is a traditional Chinese herb known for its neuroprotective and anti-inflammatory properties. It helps protect brain cells by supporting mitochondrial function and improving the health of neurons.

8. CognatiQ Whole Coffee Fruit Extract

  • CognatiQ is an innovative extract derived from the whole coffee fruit, which has been shown to increase Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor (BDNF). BDNF is a neuroprotein crucial for brain health, promoting the growth, maintenance, and survival of neurons. By boosting BDNF levels, CognatiQ enhances memory, attention, and focus, while also supporting overall brain vitality. 

9. Huperzine A

  • Huperzine A is a natural compound derived from Chinese club moss. It is known for its ability to enhance cognitive function by increasing the levels of acetylcholine, a neurotransmitter involved in memory and learning. Huperzine A acts as a powerful acetylcholinesterase inhibitor, which helps prevent the breakdown of acetylcholine in the brain.

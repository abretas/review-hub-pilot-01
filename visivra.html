Visivra Ingredients

Visivra contains six key nutrients, each selected for a specific role in supporting eye wellness. The formula combines macular-supporting pigments, antioxidant support, and essential nutrients that work together to maintain visual comfort, retinal function, and overall eye health.

Lutein & Zeaxanthin – The Macular Pigment Pair

Lutein and zeaxanthin are carotenoid pigments naturally found in the macula, the part of the retina responsible for sharp and detailed vision. They help absorb certain wavelengths of light, support the cells involved in fine detail, and maintain healthy visual contrast. Since the body does not produce these pigments, they must come from dietary sources or supplementation.

Bilberry Extract – Eye Circulation Support

Bilberry is a berry rich in anthocyanins, plant compounds associated with antioxidant activity. It has traditionally been linked with supporting healthy circulation in the eyes and helping maintain visual performance in low-light conditions.

Astaxanthin – Antioxidant Support

Astaxanthin is a marine carotenoid known for its antioxidant properties. It is included to support visual endurance and help the eyes manage the strain that can come from prolonged close-focus activities, including extended screen use.

Zinc & Vitamin A – Essential Eye Nutrients

Zinc and Vitamin A work together to support normal retinal function. Zinc helps transport Vitamin A to where it is needed, while Vitamin A plays an important role in producing light-sensitive pigments that support vision in lower-light conditions.

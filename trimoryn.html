Understanding the 10:1 Extract Ratio

A 10:1 extract ratio means that 10 parts of the original plant material are concentrated into 1 part of the final extract. This process removes water and other non-essential plant material while concentrating certain naturally occurring compounds.

For example, a 10:1 Cinnamon Bark Extract means approximately 10 grams of raw cinnamon bark are processed to produce 1 gram of concentrated extract. The goal is to provide a more concentrated form of the plant compared with using the raw ingredient alone.

In Trimoryn, Cinnamon Bark Extract 10:1 is included as part of the formula’s metabolic wellness blend. Concentrated extracts are commonly used in dietary supplements because they allow manufacturers to provide plant-based active components in smaller serving sizes while maintaining a targeted amount of botanical material.

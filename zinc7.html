Zinc7 Ingredients

Zinc7 delivers a potent 50mg dose of zinc through a unique proprietary blend that combines seven bioavailable forms of zinc with rosehips for enhanced effectiveness. This thoughtful formulation ensures optimal absorption and utilization throughout the body.

1. Rosehips

Rosehips are the fruit of the wild rose plant, rich in natural vitamin C and antioxidants. They help support immune health, lessen inflammation, and enhance zinc absorption. Their inclusion promotes overall skin and joint wellness while boosting the supplement’s effectiveness.

2. Zinc Citrate

Zinc Citrate is a highly absorbable form of zinc often used for immune support and digestive health. It plays a vital role in enzyme function and maintaining cellular integrity. Its good bioavailability makes it suitable for customers with sensitive stomachs.

3. Zinc Ascorbate

Zinc Ascorbate combines zinc with vitamin C, creating a synergistic compound that supports immune function and wound healing. It’s gentle on the stomach and enhances the body’s antioxidant defenses. This form also aids collagen synthesis and skin repair.

4. Zinc Glycinate

Zinc Glycinate is a chelated form of zinc bound to the amino acid glycine, which allows for superior absorption and minimal digestive discomfort. It supports cognitive function, muscle health, and hormonal balance. It’s especially helpful for those with zinc deficiencies.

5. Zinc Picolinate

Zinc Picolinate is well-known for its excellent absorption and is often recommended for addressing zinc deficiency. It plays a role in DNA repair, reproductive health, and neurological function. This form easily penetrates cell membranes for deep cellular support.

6. Zinc Methionate

Zinc Methionate is a chelated form that binds zinc with methionine, an essential amino acid. This enhances the stability and bioavailability of zinc in the bloodstream. It supports metabolic function, immune strength, and detoxification processes.

7. Zinc Orotate

Zinc Orotate is bound with orotic acid, which allows it to pass through cell membranes more efficiently than many other forms. It supports tissue regeneration and cellular repair. This form is often chosen for its potential neurological and cardiovascular benefits.

8. Zinc Acetate

Zinc Acetate is fast-acting and commonly used in lozenges for immune support during colds. It helps lessen inflammation and promotes healing. This form of zinc also plays a key role in enzyme production and skin regeneration.

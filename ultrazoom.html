Frequently Asked Questions

Is the 300× magnification real?

No, 300× magnification is not realistic. Small lenses can’t support that level. Usable zoom usually maxes out around 20–30× due to physics limitations.

Does it give 4K images?

It doesn’t provide true 4K. The term is mostly marketing. Image quality completely depends on your smartphone camera, not the lens or zoom attachment itself.

Can I see 6 miles away?

Seeing 6 miles clearly is highly unlikely. Tiny apertures and atmospheric haze lessen image clarity, especially over long distances. Marketing often overstates this ability.

Is it usable without a phone?

Yes, it works without a phone using its eyepiece. However, a phone mount is included so you can capture or zoom using your smartphone camera.

Does it have night vision?

It does not include true infrared night vision. Some claim “night vision,” but it likely refers to low-light use with digital enhancement from your phone.

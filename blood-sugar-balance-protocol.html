The Science Behind Blood Sugar Regulation

Understanding how our body controls blood sugar is key to staying healthy. The body's skill in managing glucose levels shows how well different parts work together.

At the center of this control are insulin and glucagon, made by the pancreas. Insulin helps cells take in glucose for energy or storage. Glucagon tells the liver to release glucose into the blood when levels are low.

Other hormones like cortisol and epinephrine also play a part. They help adjust blood sugar levels when we're stressed or active. The liver and kidneys are critical too, helping to balance glucose levels by storing and releasing it as needed.

When everything works right, our blood sugar stays stable and healthy. But, diet, lifestyle, and health issues can upset this balance, causing blood sugar to swing.

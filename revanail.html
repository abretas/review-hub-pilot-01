How to Use?

For optimal results, follow these simple steps to apply RevaNail:

  • Clean the Area: Start by cleaning and drying the affected nail and the surrounding area to ensure maximum absorption of the solution.
  • File the Nail Surface: Use an emery board to gently file the surface of the nail. This helps the solution penetrate more deeply and absorb more effectively.
  • Apply the Solution: Use the enclosed brush applicator to coat the affected nail. Apply the solution to both the nail surface and the cuticle area.
  • Massage: After applying the solution, use a cotton swab to gently work the liquid into the cuticle for better coverage.
  • Repeat Application: For best results, apply RevaNail four times daily, twice in the morning and twice in the afternoon.
